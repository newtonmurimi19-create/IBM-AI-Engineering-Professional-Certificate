This course focuses on practical, job-ready skills for engineering and fine-tuning Transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs).

## 🔬 Core Concepts

### Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

The course introduces PEFT approaches that allow pretrained models to be adapted to specific tasks while reducing the resources required for fine-tuning.

Key techniques include:

- **LoRA — Low-Rank Adaptation**
- **QLoRA — Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation**

### Pretrained Transformer Models

The course develops practical experience working with pretrained Transformer models and adapting them for downstream applications.
